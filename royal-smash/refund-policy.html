Because virtual coins, ammunition packs, and VIP passes are delivered and unlocked immediately upon checkout verification, completed digital purchases are final and cannot be cancelled or returned once credited to your game wallet or consumed in gameplay.

3. Eligible Cases for Replacement or Refund

While digital consumables are non-refundable once delivered and used, Varnams provides a 100% Instant Credit Restoration or Full Refund in the following verified situations:

  • Duplicate Payment: You were accidentally debited twice for the exact same pack due to a network timeout or UPI app retry.
  • Technical Non-Delivery: Money was successfully debited from your UPI / bank account (verifiable via a valid 12-digit UPI UTR number), but the virtual coins or VIP pass were not credited due to a browser crash or connection drop.

Note on Payment Authorization: Because every UPI or App Store transaction requires the account holder's private UPI PIN or device credentials, refunds cannot be granted for "change of mind", partial consumption of virtual coins, or claims that a family member used an unlocked phone where a valid UPI PIN was entered.
