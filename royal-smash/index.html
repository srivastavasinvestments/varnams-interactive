🎓 ROYAL ACADEMY: HOW TO PLAY
🎯
AIM, DRAG & DESTROY
• Aim: Touch & drag anywhere on the screen to swivel your cannon sight.
• Fire: Tap the big glowing FIRE! button or tap the 3D Blaster itself.
• Objective: Knock all cans and building blocks completely off the rotating table into the moat to win!
💣
8 UNIQUE PROJECTILES
• 🔴 Iron Ball: Precision sniper single-block knockout.
• 💣 Explosive Bomb: Massive cluster shockwave demolition.
• ⚡ Heavy Boulder: Dense 7.5kg mass that topples foundational columns.
• 🎳 Deck Roller: Ground bowling ball sweeping bottom cans clean.
• 🩴 Flying Chappal / Slipper: Aerodynamic flipping slap!
• 👢 Combat Boot: Steel-toed ground stomp shockwave.
• 💦 Hydro Splash: High-pressure water burst.
• 🚀 MIRV Rocket: Splits into 5 mini-bombs before hitting!
CUSTOM 3D BLASTER CANNONS
• Tap the BLASTERS button on the bottom tray anytime during gameplay.
• Unlock and equip 5 unique 3D Blaster designs:
  👑 Royal Sovereign Cannon (Laser Sight)
  🐉 Dragon Fire Mortar (Incendiary Shockwave)
  ⚡ Tesla Arc Blaster (Plasma Piercer)

😈
STRESS BUSTER (BOSS MODE)
